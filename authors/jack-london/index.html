Jack London

London’s Northland books put animals and people under the same pressure: cold, hunger, and the stories civilization tells about both. The Call of the Wild follows a dog out of California and into that pressure.

The Call of the Wild

1903 · Adventure · Novel

Buck, a stolen California dog, is worked north into the Yukon. London tells the story from the animal’s side of the harness.
