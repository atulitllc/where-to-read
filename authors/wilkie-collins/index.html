Author

Wilkie Collins

Wilkie Collins (1824–1889) is represented here by 21 public-domain titles, including A Fair Penitent, A Rogue's Life, and Antonina; Or, The Fall of Rome. This page is a shelf list: the books are read on Open Library, not stored here.

The Moonstone

The Moonstone

by Wilkie Collins

1868 · Mystery · Novel

The Moonstone is an early example of the modern detective novel, and established many of the ground rules of the modern genre. Its publication was started on 4 January 1868 and was completed on 8 August 1868.

No Name

No Name

by Wilkie Collins

Novel

No Name was originally serialised in Charles Dickens's magazine All the Year Round before book publication. No Name is the second of his four "great novels", released after The Woman in White and before Armadale and The Moonstone.

Man and Wife

Man and Wife

by Wilkie Collins

1870 · Novel

Man and Wife is Wilkie Collins's ninth published novel, first published in 1870. Collins increasingly used his novels to explore social abuses, which according to critics tends to detract from their qualities as fiction.

The Black Robe

The Black Robe

by Wilkie Collins

1881 · Mystery · Novel

The Black Robe, which relates the misadventures of one "Lewis Romayne", is noted for its anti-Catholic lens.

The Law and the Lady

The Law and the Lady

by Wilkie Collins

1875 · Mystery · Novel

The Law and the Lady is not quite as sensational in style as The Moonstone and The Woman in White.

Armadale

Armadale

by Wilkie Collins

1866 · Mystery · Novel

Armadale is the third of his four 'great novels' of the 1860s: after The Woman in White and No Name, and before The Moonstone.

The Queen of Hearts

The Queen of Hearts

by Wilkie Collins

1862 · Mystery · Novel

The queen of hearts is a playing card in the standard 52-card deck.

Antonina; Or, The Fall of Rome

Antonina; Or, The Fall of Rome

by Wilkie Collins

1850 · History · Novel

Antonina; Or, The Fall of Rome was completed and, after the first two publishers refused to accept it, it was issued by Richard Bentley as a three-volume book early in 1850. Antonina; Or, The Fall of Rome is the only example of Collins writing a classical romance novel; and it was met, as Collins later recalled, "with such a chorus of praise as has never been sung over me since".

Poor Miss Finch

Poor Miss Finch

by Wilkie Collins

1872 · Romance · Novel

Poor Miss Finch is a novel by Wilkie Collins published on 26 January 1872 about a young blind woman who temporarily regains her sight while finding herself in a romantic triangle with two brothers.

Blind Love

Blind Love

by Wilkie Collins

1890 · Mystery · Novel

Blind Love was completed by historian and novelist Sir Walter Besant. Collins's novel had already begun serialization in The Illustrated London News, even though the author had not yet completed it.

The Fallen Leaves

The Fallen Leaves

by Wilkie Collins

1879 · Novel

The Fallen Leaves," a 1879 novel by Wilkie Collins, explores the lives of four women, or "fallen leaves," linked by their relationships with the hero, Amelius Goldenheart, and secrets from their pasts, focusing on the complexities of relationships and social issues of the time.
