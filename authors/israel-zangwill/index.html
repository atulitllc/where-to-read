Children of the Ghetto: A Study of a Peculiar People

Children of the Ghetto: A Study of a Peculiar People

Children's books · Novel

Children of the Ghetto: A Study of a Peculiar People is loosely based on Zangwill's novel of the same name. Children of the Ghetto: A Study of a Peculiar People is a drama in four acts, each with a subtitle and its own setting.

The Melting-Pot

The Melting-Pot

1909 · Plays

The Melting-Pot depicts the life of a Russian Jewish immigrant family, the Quixanos, in the United States. David Quixano has survived a pogrom, which killed his mother and sister, and he wishes to forget this horrible event.

The Big Bow Mystery

The Big Bow Mystery

1895 · Mystery · Humor

The Big Bow Mystery was originally serialised in The Star newspaper, before being published as a novel the following year. Set in London's East End, it is one of the earliest examples of the locked-room mystery genre.
