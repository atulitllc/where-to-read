Lays of Ancient Rome

by Thomas Babington Macaulay, Baron Macaulay

1870 · Poetry · Fantasy · History

In Lays of Ancient Rome, four of these recount heroic episodes from early Roman history with strong dramatic and tragic themes, giving the collection its name. Macaulay also included two poems inspired by recent history: Ivry and The Armada.
