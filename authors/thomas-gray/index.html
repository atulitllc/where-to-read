Poetry

In An Elegy Wrote in a Country Church Yard (1751) and The Eton College Manuscript, the poem's origins are unknown, but it was partly inspired by Gray's thoughts following the death of the poet Richard West. Originally titled Stanzas Wrote in a Country Church-Yard, the poem was completed when Gray was living near the Church of St Giles, Stoke Poges.
