An Occurrence at Owl Creek Bridge

An Occurrence at Owl Creek Bridge

by Ambrose Bierce

Short stories · Gothic · History

An Occurrence at Owl Creek Bridge was originally published by the San Francisco Examiner on July 13,, and was first collected in Bierce's book Tales of Soldiers and Civilians. Bierce's abandonment of strict linear narration in favor of the internal mind of the protagonist is an early example of the stream-of-consciousness narrative mode.

The Devil's Dictionary

The Devil's Dictionary

by Ambrose Bierce

Humor

In The Devil's Dictionary, the lexicon was written over three decades as a series of installments for magazines and newspapers. Bierce's witty definitions were imitated and plagiarized for years before he gathered them into books, first as The Cynic's Word Book and then in a more complete version as The Devil's Dictionary.
