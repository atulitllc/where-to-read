Pierre; or The Ambiguities

Pierre; or The Ambiguities

by Herman Melville

Novel

In Pierre; or The Ambiguities, Pierre; or, The Ambiguities is the seventh book by American writer Herman Melville, first published in New York. Pierre; or The Ambiguities uses many conventions of Gothic fiction and depicts psychological, sexual, and familial tension between Pierre Glendinning, his widowed mother, his cousin Glendinning Stanly, his fiancée Lucy Tartan, and Isabel Banford, who is revealed to be his half-sister.
