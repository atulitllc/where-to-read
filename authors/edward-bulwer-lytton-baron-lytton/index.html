The Last Days of Pompeii

The Last Days of Pompeii

by Edward Bulwer Lytton, Baron Lytton

History · Novel

The Last Days of Pompeii was inspired by the painting The Last Day of Pompeii by the Russian painter Karl Briullov, which Bulwer-Lytton had seen in Milan. The Last Days of Pompeii culminates in the cataclysmic destruction of the city of Pompeii by the eruption of Mount Vesuvius in AD 79.

The Lady of Lyons; Or, Love and Pride

The Lady of Lyons; Or, Love and Pride

by Edward Bulwer Lytton, Baron Lytton

1838 · Plays

The Lady of Lyons; or, Love and Pride, commonly known as The Lady of Lyons, is a five-act romantic melodrama written in 1838 by Edward Bulwer-Lytton, 1st Baron Lytton. The Lady of Lyons; Or, Love and Pride was first produced in London at Covent Garden Theatre on 15 February 1838 and was revived many times over the rest of the 19th century.
