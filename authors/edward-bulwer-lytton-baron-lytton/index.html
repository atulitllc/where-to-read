The Last Days of Pompeii

The Last Days of Pompeii

by Edward Bulwer Lytton, Baron Lytton

History · Novel

The Last Days of Pompeii was inspired by the painting The Last Day of Pompeii by the Russian painter Karl Briullov, which Bulwer-Lytton had seen in Milan. The Last Days of Pompeii culminates in the cataclysmic destruction of the city of Pompeii by the eruption of Mount Vesuvius in AD 79.

The Lady of Lyons; Or, Love and Pride

The Lady of Lyons; Or, Love and Pride

by Edward Bulwer Lytton, Baron Lytton

1838 · Plays

The Lady of Lyons; or, Love and Pride, commonly known as The Lady of Lyons, is a five-act romantic melodrama written in 1838 by Edward Bulwer-Lytton, 1st Baron Lytton. The Lady of Lyons; Or, Love and Pride was first produced in London at Covent Garden Theatre on 15 February 1838 and was revived many times over the rest of the 19th century.

Eugene Aram — Volume 01

Eugene Aram — Volume 01

by Edward Bulwer Lytton, Baron Lytton

Mystery · Novel

It depicts the events leading up to the execution of Eugene Aram in for murdering his business partner.

Pelham — Volume 03

Pelham — Volume 03

by Edward Bulwer Lytton, Baron Lytton

Adventure · History · Novel

Pelham was his breakthrough novel, launching him as one of Britain's leading authors. Pelham is part of the tradition of silver fork novels that enjoyed great popularity in the late Regency and early Victorian eras.

Ernest Maltravers — Volume 02

Ernest Maltravers — Volume 02

by Edward Bulwer Lytton, Baron Lytton

Romance · Novel

Ernest Maltravers is Gothic in style, and features a protagonist combining "Byronic stature and Coleridgean philosophical ambition". The following year it was adapted into a stage play of the same title by Louisa Medina, which first appeared on 28 March at the National Theatre in New York City.

Kenelm Chillingly — Volume 01

Kenelm Chillingly — Volume 01

by Edward Bulwer Lytton, Baron Lytton

Novel

Kenelm Chillingly was published in three volumes by William Blackwood. Kenelm Chillingly was published the same year as his death and was his penultimate work before the posthumous The Parisians.

Paul Clifford — Volume 04

Paul Clifford — Volume 04

by Edward Bulwer Lytton, Baron Lytton

Novel

Paul Clifford tells the life of Paul Clifford, a man who leads a dual life as both a criminal and an upscale gentleman. Paul Clifford was successful upon its release.

Godolphin, Volume 1

Godolphin, Volume 1

by Edward Bulwer Lytton, Baron Lytton

Novel

It concerns the life of an idealistic man, Percy Godolphin, and his eventual lover, Constance Vernon. Written as a frame narrative, Godolphin provides a satirical insight into the day-to-day lives of the early 19th-century British elite.

Leila or, the Siege of Granada, Book I

Leila or, the Siege of Granada, Book I

by Edward Bulwer Lytton, Baron Lytton

Romance · History · Novel

Leila or, the Siege of Granada, Book I is set in Granada, Spain at the end of the Middle Ages — beginning in the summer. Leila or, the Siege of Granada, Book I was originally published in an expensive form, with many engraved illustrations.
