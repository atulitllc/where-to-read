Niccolò Machiavelli

Machiavelli’s The Prince is a short, blunt book about getting and keeping power. It has been read as advice, as satire, and as a report on how states actually behave.

The Prince

The Prince

1532 · Philosophy

A short treatise on acquiring and holding a state, written in exile and dedicated to a Medici. Machiavelli prefers what rulers do to what mirror-books said they should do.
