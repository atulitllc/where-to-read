A Strange Manuscript Found in a Copper Cylinder

A Strange Manuscript Found in a Copper Cylinder

by James De Mille

1888 · Science fiction · Adventure · Novel

A Strange Manuscript Found in a Copper Cylinder was serialized posthumously and anonymously in Harper's Weekly, and published in book form by Harper and Brothers of New York City in 1888. A Strange Manuscript Found in a Copper Cylinder was serialized subsequently in the United Kingdom and Australia, and published in book form in the United Kingdom and Canada.
