by Jules Verne

Science fiction · Fantasy · Adventure

In The Field of Ice Part II of the Adventures of Captain Hatteras, Captain Nemo ( NAY-moh; also known as Prince Dakkar) is a character created by the French novelist Jules Verne (1828–1905). Nemo appears in two of Verne's science-fiction books, Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Seas and The Mysterious Island.

Facing the Flag

Facing the Flag

by Jules Verne

Science fiction · Fantasy · Adventure

Facing the Flag is part of the Voyages extraordinaires series. Like The Begum's Millions, which Verne published, it has the theme of France and the entire world threatened by a super-weapon with the threat finally overcome through the force of French patriotism.

All Around the Moon

All Around the Moon

by Jules Verne

Science fiction · Fantasy · Adventure

Around the Moon (French: Autour de la Lune, ), also translated as Circling the Moon and All Around the Moon, is the sequel to Jules Verne's novel, From the Earth to the Moon. All Around the Moon is a science fiction tale which continues the trip to the Moon that was only begun in the first novel.

A Journey to the Centre of the Earth

A Journey to the Centre of the Earth

by Jules Verne

Science fiction · Fantasy · Adventure

A Journey to the Centre of the Earth was first published in French, then reissued a revised and expanded edition. Professor Otto Lidenbrock is the tale's central figure, an eccentric German scientist who decodes a message from a 16th century Icelandic alchemist who claims to have reached to the very center of the earth via volcanic tubes in the crater of the celebrated inactive Icelandic volcano Snæfellsjökull.

In A Floating City, and The Blockade Runners, "The Blockade Runners" (French: Les forceurs de blocus) is a novella by Jules Verne. In it was published in single volume together with novel A Floating City as a part of the Voyages Extraordinaires series (The Extraordinary Voyages).
