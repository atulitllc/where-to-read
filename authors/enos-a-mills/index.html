The Rocky Mountain Wonderland

The Rocky Mountain Wonderland

by Enos A. Mills

1915 · History · Travel · Science

In The Rocky Mountain Wonderland, History of Rocky Mountain National Park began when Paleo-Indians traveled along what is now Trail Ridge Road to hunt and forage for food. Ute and Arapaho people subsequently hunted and camped in the area.

In Beaver World

In Beaver World

by Enos A. Mills

1913 · Literature

In Beaver World: the Beaver Mills Lumber Company was a sawmill established the Rainy River in Northwestern Ontario, near the international border with Baudette, Minnesota. In, as the Rat Portage Lumber Company, it was destroyed in the Baudette Fire.

Watched by Wild Animals

Watched by Wild Animals

by Enos A. Mills

1922 · History · Travel · Science

In Watched by Wild Animals, Humane World for Animals, formerly the Humane Society of the United States (HSUS) and Humane Society International (HSI), is a global nonprofit organization that focuses on animal welfare and opposes animal-related cruelties of national scope.
