The Divorce of Catherine of Aragon The Story as Told by the Imperial Ambassadors Resident at the Court of Henry VIII

History

In The Divorce of Catherine of Aragon The Story as Told by the Imperial Ambassadors Resident at the Court of Henry VIII, Catherine of Aragon (also spelled Katherine; Spanish: Catalina de Aragón; 16 December – 7 January ) was Queen of England as the first wife of Henry VIII from their marriage on 11 June until its annulment on 23 May. She had previously been Princess of Wales as the wife of Henry's elder brother Arthur, Prince of Wales for a short time before his death.
