Vanity Fair

Vanity Fair

by William Makepeace Thackeray

History · Humor · Novel

Vanity Fair was published as a single volume in with the subtitle A Novel without a Hero. Vanity Fair is sometimes considered the "principal founder" of the Victorian domestic novel.

The Second Funeral of Napoleon

by William Makepeace Thackeray

1841 · History

In The Second Funeral of Napoleon, Written under the pseudonym of Michael Angelo Titmarsh, this essay describes the events of December 15, as Thackeray witnessed the celebrations connected with the arrival of Napoleon's body from St. Helena for burial in Paris.

Barry Lyndon

Barry Lyndon

by William Makepeace Thackeray

History · Humor · Novel

Thackeray, who based the novel on the life and exploits of the Anglo-Irish rake and fortune-hunter Andrew Robinson Stoney, later reissued it under the title The Memoirs of Barry Lyndon, Esq. Barry Lyndon is narrated by Lyndon himself, who functions as a quintessentially unreliable narrator.
