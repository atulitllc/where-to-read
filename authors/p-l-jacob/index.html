The Arts in the Middle Ages and at the Period of the Renaissance

by P. L. Jacob

1870 · History

In The Arts in the Middle Ages and at the Period of the Renaissance, the Renaissance (UK: rin-AY-sənss, US: REN-ə-sahnss, "rebirth") was an European period of history and cultural movement taking place at the end of the Late Middle Ages and the beginning of the early modern era. The Arts in the Middle Ages and at the Period of the Renaissance is variously defined as covering the 15th and 16th centuries or, more broadly, as lasting from the 14th century to the 17th.
