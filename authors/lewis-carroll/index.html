The Game of Logic

The Game of Logic

by Lewis Carroll

1887 · Children's books · Science

In The Game of Logic, in addition to his well-known children's literature, Dodgson/Carroll was an academic mathematician who worked in mathematical logic. The Game of Logic describes, in an informal and playful style, the use of a board game to represent logical propositions and inferences.

Condensation of Determinants Being a New and Brief Method for Computing their Arithmetical Values

Condensation of Determinants Being a New and Brief Method for Computing their Arithmetical Values

by Lewis Carroll

Science

In Condensation of Determinants Being a New and Brief Method for Computing their Arithmetical Values, in mathematics, Dodgson condensation or method of contractants is a method of computing the determinants of square matrices. Condensation of Determinants Being a New and Brief Method for Computing their Arithmetical Values is named for its inventor, Charles Lutwidge Dodgson (better known by his pseudonym, as Lewis Carroll, the popular author), who discovered it.
