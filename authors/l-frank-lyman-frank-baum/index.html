Author

L. Frank (Lyman Frank) Baum

L. Frank (Lyman Frank) Baum (1856–1919) is represented here by 27 public-domain titles, including American Fairy Tales, Aunt Jane's Nieces, and Aunt Jane's Nieces Abroad. This page is a shelf list: the books are read on Open Library, not stored here.

The Magic of Oz

The Magic of Oz

by L. Frank (Lyman Frank) Baum

Science fiction · Fantasy · Adventure

Published on June 7, 1919, one month after the author's death, The Magic of Oz relates the unsuccessful attempt of the Munchkin boy Kiki Aru and former Nome King Ruggedo to conquer Oz.

Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

by L. Frank (Lyman Frank) Baum

1908 · Science fiction · Fantasy · Adventure

Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz was published on June 18, 1908 and reunites Dorothy Gale with the humbug Wizard from The Wonderful Wizard of Oz. This is one of only two of the original fourteen Oz books to be illustrated with watercolor paintings.

The Road to Oz

The Road to Oz

by L. Frank (Lyman Frank) Baum

Science fiction · Fantasy · Adventure

The Road to Oz was originally published and documents the adventures of Dorothy Gale's fourth visit to the Land of Oz. The Road to Oz was followed by The Emerald City of Oz.

Tik-Tok of Oz

Tik-Tok of Oz

by L. Frank (Lyman Frank) Baum

1914 · Science fiction · Fantasy · Children's books

Tik-Tok of Oz has little to do with Tik-Tok and is primarily the quest of the Shaggy Man (introduced in The Road to Oz) to rescue his brother, and his resulting conflict with the Nome King. Tik-Tok of Oz was based on Baum's play The Tik-Tok Man of Oz, which was produced in Los Angeles in spring.

Aunt Jane's Nieces on the Ranch

Aunt Jane's Nieces on the Ranch

by L. Frank (Lyman Frank) Baum

Mystery · Children's books · Novel

Aunt Jane's Nieces on the Ranch depicts a story of racial tension on the California ranch owned by the progressive-minded Arthur Weldon and Louise Merrick Weldon, who have entrusted their baby, Jane, nicknamed "Toodlums," to a Mexican governess named Inez.

The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

by L. Frank (Lyman Frank) Baum

Fantasy · Adventure · Children's books

The Wonderful Wizard of Oz is the first novel in the Oz series of books. A Kansas farm girl named Dorothy ends up in the magical Land of Oz after she and her pet dog Toto are swept away from their home by a cyclone.
