Alexandre Dumas

Dumas built The Count of Monte Cristo on wrongful imprisonment, patience, and a fortune used as a plot. It is a long entertainment that keeps its eye on revenge as a life’s work.

The Count of Monte Cristo

The Count of Monte Cristo

by Alexandre Dumas

1844 · Novel · Adventure

Edmond Dantès is imprisoned on a false charge and comes back, years later, with a new name and a plan. Dumas makes patience itself into an adventure.

The black tulip

The black tulip

by Alexandre Dumas

History · Novel

The Black Tulip is a historical novel and a work of Romantic poetry written by Alexandre Dumas, père, and first published.

The Vicomte de Bragelonne

The Vicomte de Bragelonne

by Alexandre Dumas

Adventure · History · Novel

The Vicomte de Bragelonne is the third and last of The d'Artagnan Romances, following The Three Musketeers and Twenty Years After. The Vicomte de Bragelonne appeared first in serial form.

The Companions of Jehu

The Companions of Jehu

by Alexandre Dumas

Adventure · History · Novel

It is inspired by the story of the Companions of Jehu, a group of Royalist vigilantes during the French Revolution. The Companions of Jehu was also turned into a play by Dumas and Charles Gabet premiering at the Théâtre de la Gaîté on 2 July.

The queen's necklace

The queen's necklace

by Alexandre Dumas

History · Novel

The queen's necklace is loosely based on the Affair of the Diamond Necklace, an episode involving fraud and royal scandal that made headlines at the court of Louis XVI in the 1780s.

The regent's daughter

The regent's daughter

by Alexandre Dumas

History · Novel

The regent's daughter has sometimes been subtitled as a sequel to The Conspirators.

Le chevalier de Maison-Rouge

Le chevalier de Maison-Rouge

by Alexandre Dumas

History · Novel

Le chevalier de Maison-Rouge was translated into English in under the title The Knight of Redcastle. Le chevalier de Maison-Rouge has also been translated as The Knight of Maison-Rouge: A Novel of Marie Antoinette.
