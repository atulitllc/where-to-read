The Idle Thoughts of an Idle Fellow

The Idle Thoughts of an Idle Fellow

by Jerome K. (Jerome Klapka) Jerome

Humor · Letters

The Idle Thoughts of an Idle Fellow was the author’s second published book and it helped establish him as a leading English humorist. While widely considered one of Jerome’s better works, and in spite of using the same style as Three Men in a Boat, it was never as popular as the latter.

Diary of a Pilgrimage

Diary of a Pilgrimage

by Jerome K. (Jerome Klapka) Jerome

1891 · History · Humor · Travel

Diary of a Pilgrimage tells of Jerome's first visit to Germany, a trip he undertakes with his friend "B" to see the Oberammergau Passion Play.

Three men on the bummel

Three men on the bummel

by Jerome K. (Jerome Klapka) Jerome

1900 · Humor · Novel

Three men on the bummel was published in 1900, eleven years after his most famous work, Three Men in a Boat. The sequel brings back the three companions who figured in Three Men in a Boat, this time on a bicycle tour through Germany.
