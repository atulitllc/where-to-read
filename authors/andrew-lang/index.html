The Yellow Fairy Book

The Yellow Fairy Book

by Andrew Lang

1906 · Fantasy · Children's books

In The Yellow Fairy Book, First published, this extensive fairy tale anthology was edited by Andrew Lang, a pioneering author and critic. By bringing together folk tales from all over the world and having them translated into English, many for the first time, he was able to create rich and varied collections of stories, opening readers' eyes to a whole world of magical possibility overseas.

John Knox and the Reformation

History

In John Knox and the Reformation, John Knox (c. – 24 November ) was a Scottish minister, Reformed theologian, and writer who was a leader of the country's Reformation. He was the reformer of the Church of Scotland.

James VI and the Gowrie Mystery

James VI and the Gowrie Mystery

by Andrew Lang

Mystery · History

In James VI and the Gowrie Mystery, John Ruthven, 3rd Earl of Gowrie (c. – 5 August ) was a Scottish nobleman who died in mysterious circumstances, referred to as the "Gowrie Conspiracy", in which he and/or his brother Alexander were attempting to kill or kidnap King James VI of Scotland for unknown purposes.

Magic and Religion

Magic and Religion

by Andrew Lang

Philosophy · Religion

In Magic and Religion, People who believe in magic can be found in all societies, regardless of whether they have organized religious hierarchies, including formal clergy, or more informal systems. Such concepts tend to appear more frequently in cultures based in polytheism, animism, or shamanism.
