The Coral Island

The Coral Island

by R. M. (Robert Michael) Ballantyne

Adventure · Children's books · History

In The Coral Island, one of the first works of juvenile fiction to feature exclusively juvenile heroes, the story relates the adventures of three boys marooned on a South Pacific island, the only survivors of a shipwreck. A typical Robinsonade – a genre of fiction inspired by Daniel Defoe's Robinson Crusoe – and one of the most popular of its type, the book first went on sale in late and has never been out of print.

The Gorilla Hunters

The Gorilla Hunters

by R. M. (Robert Michael) Ballantyne

1860 · Adventure · Children's books · History

In The Gorilla Hunters, a sequel to his hugely successful novel The Coral Island and set in "darkest Africa", its main characters are the earlier novel's three boys: Ralph, Peterkin and Jack. The Gorilla Hunters's themes are similar to those of The Coral Island, in which the boys testify to the positive influence of missionary work among the natives.
