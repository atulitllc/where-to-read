Where to Read / Jane Austen

Author

Jane Austen

Austen wrote six finished novels about money, rank, and judgment in English gentry life. The wit is social rather than sentimental: people reveal themselves by what they notice.

Pride and Prejudice

Pride and Prejudice

1813 · Novel · Romance

Elizabeth Bennet and Mr. Darcy misjudge each other in a comedy of marriage, income, and pride. Austen’s second published novel is still the surest entrance to her shelf.

Emma

Emma

1815 · Novel · Romance

Emma Woodhouse has money, leisure, and a talent for arranging other people’s lives. The novel lets her be charming and wrong in the same sentence.

Sense and Sensibility

Sense and Sensibility

1811 · Novel · Romance

Two sisters, turned out of their home, try sense and feeling as ways of surviving love and reduced means. It was Austen’s first published novel.

Persuasion

Persuasion

1817 · Novel · Romance

Anne Elliot broke an engagement years ago and has had to live quietly with the decision. A return of the man she refused gives the book its second chance.

Northanger Abbey

Northanger Abbey

Gothic · Romance · Humor

The honest line on Northanger Abbey is that it is about satire and appreciation. A writer of the eighteenth century, Jane Austen (1775–1817), is the author a reader meets on the gothic, romance, and humor shelves.

Mansfield Park

Mansfield Park

Romance · Children's books · Novel

Mansfield Park is occupied with children of the rich and cousins. The eighteenth-century writer Jane Austen (1775–1817) is simply the writer, with the book kept on the romance, children's books, and novel shelves.

Lady Susan

Lady Susan

Novel

Lady Susan is built as a novel around widows and mothers and daughters. A writer of the eighteenth century, Jane Austen (1775–1817), is the writer, and the book sits on the novel shelf.

Love and Freindship [sic]

Love and Freindship [sic]

Romance · Humor · Letters

Look past the binding and Love and Freindship [sic] is still about daily life in England. The eighteenth-century writer Jane Austen (1775–1817) is the person behind the book on the romance, humor, and letters shelves.

Sense and Sensibility

Sense and Sensibility

Romance · Novel

A plain note on Sense and Sensibility: it is about inheritance and succession and sisters. The eighteenth-century writer Jane Austen (1775–1817) keeps company with the other books on the romance and novel shelves.

The Letters of Jane Austen Selected from the compilation of her great nephew, Edward, Lord Bradbourne

The Letters of Jane Austen Selected from the compilation of her great nephew, Edward, Lord Bradbourne

Letters

Open The Letters of Jane Austen Selected from the compilation of her great nephew, Edward, Lord Bradbourne expecting what the title itself names. The eighteenth-century writer Jane Austen (1775–1817) is enough authorship for the letters shelf.
