The Chouans

The Chouans

History · Novel

In The Chouans, Set in the French region of Brittany, the novel combines military history with a love story between the aristocratic Marie de Verneuil and the Chouan royalist Alphonse de Montauran. The Chouans takes place during the 1799 post-war uprising in Fougères.

Rise and Fall of Cesar Birotteau

In Rise and Fall of Cesar Birotteau, the main character is a Parisian perfumer, with the prototype being a legendary perfumer, Jean Vincent Bully, who achieves success in the cosmetics business, but becomes bankrupt due to property speculation.

Beatrix

Beatrix

Novel

Beatrix first appeared in the periodical Le Siècle in August, and appeared in volume form the same year. Balzac based the characters in this novel on real figures: Félicité des Touches, a celebrated musician and writer, is based on George Sand.
