Love and Intrigue: A Tragedy

Love and Intrigue: A Tragedy

Plays

In Love and Intrigue: A Tragedy, his third play, it was first performed on 13 April at Schauspiel Frankfurt. The play shows how cabals and their intrigue destroy the love between Ferdinand von Walter, a nobleman's son, and Luise Miller, daughter of a middle-class musician.

The Death of Wallenstein

The Death of Wallenstein

Plays · History

In The Death of Wallenstein, Wallenstein is the popular designation of a trilogy of dramas by German author Friedrich Schiller. The Death of Wallenstein consists of the plays Wallenstein's Camp (Wallensteins Lager), a lengthy prologue, The Piccolomini (Die Piccolomini), and Wallenstein's Death (Wallensteins Tod).

Mary Stuart: A Tragedy

Mary Stuart: A Tragedy

In Mary Stuart: A Tragedy, the play consists of five acts, each divided into several scenes. The play had its première in Weimar, Germany on 14 June.
