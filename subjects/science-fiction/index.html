Where to Read / Science fiction

Subject

Science fiction

Early speculative fiction about time, invention, and other worlds. The ideas travel; the editions on this shelf are the old ones, not modern retellings.

Frankenstein

Frankenstein

by Mary Shelley

1818 · Novel · Gothic · Science fiction

Victor Frankenstein builds a living being and then abandons it. The creature’s education, and Victor’s refusal to answer for what he made, are the book’s real experiment.

The Time Machine

The Time Machine

by H. G. Wells

1895 · Science fiction · Novel

A Victorian inventor rides his machine to a future in which humanity has split. Wells’s novella is a social argument dressed as a traveler’s report.

The War of the Worlds

The War of the Worlds

by H. G. Wells

1898 · Science fiction · Novel

Martian cylinders fall on the English countryside and the narrator walks through the collapse of ordinary life. Wells aimed the invasion at the empire that had invaded others.

A Connecticut Yankee in King Arthur's Court

A Connecticut Yankee in King Arthur's Court

by Mark Twain

Science fiction · Fantasy · Humor

A Connecticut Yankee in King Arthur's Court was originally titled A Yankee in King Arthur's Court. Some early editions are titled A Yankee at the Court of King Arthur.

At the Back of the North Wind

At the Back of the North Wind

by George MacDonald

Science fiction · Fantasy · Children's books

At the Back of the North Wind was serialized in the children's magazine Good Words for the Young beginning and was published in book form. At the Back of the North Wind is a fantasy centered on a boy named Diamond and his adventures with the North Wind.

Before Adam

Before Adam

by Jack London

Science fiction · Fantasy · Adventure

Before Adam recounts a man who dreams he lives the life of an early hominid. Before Adam offers an early view of human evolution.

Phantastes: A Faerie Romance for Men and Women

Phantastes: A Faerie Romance for Men and Women

by George MacDonald

Science fiction · Fantasy · Romance

Phantastes: A Faerie Romance for Men and Women turns on the character Anodos ("pathless" in Greek) and takes its inspiration from German Romanticism, particularly Novalis. Phantastes: A Faerie Romance for Men and Women concerns a young man who is pulled into a dreamlike world and there hunts for his ideal of female beauty, embodied by the "Marble Lady".

The Magic of Oz

The Magic of Oz

by L. Frank (Lyman Frank) Baum

Science fiction · Fantasy · Adventure

Published on June 7, 1919, one month after the author's death, The Magic of Oz relates the unsuccessful attempt of the Munchkin boy Kiki Aru and former Nome King Ruggedo to conquer Oz.

Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz

by L. Frank (Lyman Frank) Baum

1908 · Science fiction · Fantasy · Adventure

Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz was published on June 18, 1908 and reunites Dorothy Gale with the humbug Wizard from The Wonderful Wizard of Oz. This is one of only two of the original fourteen Oz books to be illustrated with watercolor paintings.

The Road to Oz

The Road to Oz

by L. Frank (Lyman Frank) Baum

Science fiction · Fantasy · Adventure

The Road to Oz was originally published and documents the adventures of Dorothy Gale's fourth visit to the Land of Oz. The Road to Oz was followed by The Emerald City of Oz.

Sylvie and Bruno

Sylvie and Bruno

by Lewis Carroll

Science fiction · Fantasy · Children's books

Sylvie and Bruno, first published, and its second volume Sylvie and Bruno Concluded published, form the last novel by Lewis Carroll published during his lifetime. Both volumes were illustrated by Harry Furniss.

Looking Backward, 2000 to 1887

Looking Backward, 2000 to 1887

by Edward Bellamy

Science fiction · Fantasy · Travel

Looking Backward, 2000 to 1887 was translated into several languages, and in short order "sold a million copies. " According to historian Daniel Immerwahr, "In the 19th-century United States, only Uncle Tom's Cabin sold more copies in its first years" than Bellamy's book.

The Light Princess

The Light Princess

by George MacDonald

Science fiction · Fantasy · Children's books

The Light Princess was published in as a story within the larger story Adela Cathcart. Drawing on inspiration from the story "Sleeping Beauty", it recounts a princess afflicted by a constant weightlessness, unable to get her feet on the ground, both literally and metaphorically, until she finds a love that brings her down to earth.

The Princess and Curdie

The Princess and Curdie

by George MacDonald

Science fiction · Fantasy · Children's books

The Princess and Curdie is the sequel to The Princess and the Goblin. The adventure continues with Princess Irene and Curdie a year or two older.

Five Children and It

Five Children and It

by E. (Edith) Nesbit

Science fiction · Fantasy · Children's books

Five Children and It was originally published the Strand Magazine under the general title The Psammead, or the Gifts, with a segment appearing each month. The stories were then expanded into a novel which was published the same year.

The Red One

The Red One

by Jack London

Short stories · Science fiction · Fantasy

The Red One was first published in the October issue of The Cosmopolitan, two years after London's death. The Red One was reprinted in the same year by MacMillan, in a gathering of London's stories of the same name.

The Phoenix and the Carpet

The Phoenix and the Carpet

by E. (Edith) Nesbit

Science fiction · Fantasy · Children's books

The Phoenix and the Carpet is the second in a trilogy of novels that begins with Five Children and It, and follows the adventures of the same five children: Cyril, Anthea, Robert, Jane and the Lamb. Their mother buys the children a new carpet to replace one from the nursery that they destroyed in an accidental fire.

The Story of the Amulet

The Story of the Amulet

by E. (Edith) Nesbit

Science fiction · Fantasy · Adventure

The Story of the Amulet is the final part of a trilogy of novels and is preceded byFive Children and It and The Phoenix and the Carpet.

Tik-Tok of Oz

Tik-Tok of Oz

by L. Frank (Lyman Frank) Baum

1914 · Science fiction · Fantasy · Children's books

Tik-Tok of Oz has little to do with Tik-Tok and is primarily the quest of the Shaggy Man (introduced in The Road to Oz) to rescue his brother, and his resulting conflict with the Nome King. Tik-Tok of Oz was based on Baum's play The Tik-Tok Man of Oz, which was produced in Los Angeles in spring.

Extract from Captain Stormfield's Visit to Heaven

Extract from Captain Stormfield's Visit to Heaven

by Mark Twain

Science fiction · Fantasy · Humor

Extract from Captain Stormfield's Visit to Heaven first appeared in print in Harper's Magazine in December and January, and was published in book form with some revisions. This was the last story published by Twain during his life.

The iron heel

The iron heel

by Jack London

Science fiction · Fantasy · Novel

The Iron Heel is a dystopian and political novel in the form of science fiction by American writer Jack London, first published.

The Lair of the White Worm

The Lair of the White Worm

by Bram Stoker

Science fiction · Gothic · Fantasy

The Lair of the White Worm was first published by Rider and Son of London, the year before Stoker's death, with colour illustrations by Pamela Colman Smith. The Lair of the White Worm is based on the legend of the Lambton Worm.

A Journey in Other Worlds: A Romance of the Future

A Journey in Other Worlds: A Romance of the Future

by John Jacob Astor

Science fiction · Fantasy · Romance

A Journey in Other Worlds: A Romance of the Future is a science fiction novel by John Jacob Astor IV, published.

Lilith: A Romance

Lilith: A Romance

by George MacDonald

Science fiction · Fantasy · Romance

Lilith: A Romance is considered among the darkest of MacDonald's works, and among the most profound. In the story, MacDonald mentions a cosmic sleep that heals tortured souls, preceding the salvation of all.

The History of Caliph Vathek

The History of Caliph Vathek

by William Beckford

Science fiction · Gothic · Fantasy

The History of Caliph Vathek was composed in French beginning, and then translated into English by Reverend Samuel Henley in which form it was first published in without Beckford's name as An Arabian Tale, From an Unpublished Manuscript, claiming to be translated directly from Arabic.

New Atlantis

New Atlantis

by Francis Bacon

Science fiction · Fantasy · Novel

New Atlantis appeared unheralded and tucked into the back of a longer work of natural history, Sylva Sylvarum ("Forest of Materials"). In New Atlantis, Bacon portrays a vision of the future of human discovery and knowledge, expressing his aspirations and ideals for humankind.

The Wood Beyond the World

The Wood Beyond the World

by William Morris

Science fiction · Fantasy · Adventure

The Wood Beyond the World was first published in hardcover by Morris's Kelmscott Press,. The Ballantine edition includes an introduction by Lin Carter.

The Pursuit of the House-Boat

The Pursuit of the House-Boat

by John Kendrick Bangs

1897 · Science fiction · Mystery · Fantasy

The original full title was The Pursuit of the House-Boat: Being Some Further Account of the Divers Doings of the Associated Shades, Under the Leadership of Sherlock Holmes, Esq. And it has also been titled In Pursuit of the House-Boat and Pursuit of the House-Boat.

The Enchanted Castle

The Enchanted Castle

by E. (Edith) Nesbit

Science fiction · Fantasy · Adventure

The Enchanted Castle is a children's fantasy novel by Edith Nesbit first published.

She and Allan

She and Allan

by H. Rider (Henry Rider) Haggard

1921 · Science fiction · Fantasy · Adventure

She and Allan brought together his two most popular characters, Ayesha from his novel She (to which it serves as a prequel), and Allan Quatermain from his novel King Solomon's Mines. Umslopogaas from Nada the Lily also appears in the novel as a major character.

A Strange Manuscript Found in a Copper Cylinder

A Strange Manuscript Found in a Copper Cylinder

by James De Mille

1888 · Science fiction · Adventure · Novel

A Strange Manuscript Found in a Copper Cylinder was serialized posthumously and anonymously in Harper's Weekly, and published in book form by Harper and Brothers of New York City in 1888. A Strange Manuscript Found in a Copper Cylinder was serialized subsequently in the United Kingdom and Australia, and published in book form in the United Kingdom and Canada.

The Consolidator; or, Memoirs of Sundry Transactions from the World in the Moon

The Consolidator; or, Memoirs of Sundry Transactions from the World in the Moon

by Daniel Defoe

Science fiction · Fantasy · Humor

The Consolidator; or, Memoirs of Sundry Transactions from the World in the Moon is a fictional adventure by Daniel Defoe published. The Consolidator; or, Memoirs of Sundry Transactions from the World in the Moon is a satirical novel that mixes fantasy, political satire, and social satire.

The Story of Ab: A Tale of the Time of the Cave Man

The Story of Ab: A Tale of the Time of the Cave Man

by Stanley Waterloo

1901 · Science fiction · Adventure · History

The Story of Ab, or The Story of Ab: A Tale of the Time of the Cave Man, is a novel written by Stanley Waterloo.

The House on the Borderland

The House on the Borderland

by William Hope Hodgson

Science fiction · Mystery · Gothic

The House on the Borderland is a hallucinatory account of a recluse's stay at a remote house, and his experiences of supernatural creatures and otherworldly dimensions. Lovecraft praised The House on the Borderland and other works by Hodgson at length.

Across the Zodiac The Story of a Wrecked Record

Across the Zodiac The Story of a Wrecked Record

by Percy Greg

Science fiction · Fantasy · Novel

Across the Zodiac The Story of a Wrecked Record is the first science fiction novel set primarily on Mars. Across the Zodiac The Story of a Wrecked Record contains the first documented use of the term "astronaut", here the name of a spacecraft.

Facing the Flag

Facing the Flag

by Jules Verne

Science fiction · Fantasy · Adventure

Facing the Flag is part of the Voyages extraordinaires series. Like The Begum's Millions, which Verne published, it has the theme of France and the entire world threatened by a super-weapon with the threat finally overcome through the force of French patriotism.

All Around the Moon

All Around the Moon

by Jules Verne

Science fiction · Fantasy · Adventure

Around the Moon (French: Autour de la Lune, ), also translated as Circling the Moon and All Around the Moon, is the sequel to Jules Verne's novel, From the Earth to the Moon. All Around the Moon is a science fiction tale which continues the trip to the Moon that was only begun in the first novel.

The Last Man

The Last Man

by Mary Wollstonecraft Shelley

Science fiction · Fantasy · Novel

The Last Man concerns Europe in the late 21st century, ravaged by the rise of a bubonic plague pandemic that rapidly sweeps across the entire globe, ultimately resulting in the near-extinction of humanity. The Last Man also includes discussion of the British state as a republic, for which Shelley sat in meetings of the House of Commons to gain insight to the governmental system of the Romantic era.

A Journey to the Centre of the Earth

A Journey to the Centre of the Earth

by Jules Verne

Science fiction · Fantasy · Adventure

A Journey to the Centre of the Earth was first published in French, then reissued a revised and expanded edition. Professor Otto Lidenbrock is the tale's central figure, an eccentric German scientist who decodes a message from a 16th century Icelandic alchemist who claims to have reached to the very center of the earth via volcanic tubes in the crater of the celebrated inactive Icelandic volcano Snæfellsjökull.

The discovery of a world in the moone

The discovery of a world in the moone

by John Wilkins

1638 · Science fiction · Fantasy · Philosophy

The discovery of a world in the moone received generally good reviews as a showcase for the band's experimental/psychedelic pop. The album is the band's first major change in production style compared to earlier albums.

The Mummy's Foot

The Mummy's Foot

by Théophile Gautier

Short stories · Science fiction · Gothic

The Mummy's Foot relates the fantastical tale of a contemporary man and the adventures which befall him when he ventures into a Parisian curiosity shop and buys the four-thousand-year-old foot of Princess Hermonthis.

To Whom This May Come 1898

To Whom This May Come 1898

by Edward Bellamy

Short stories · Science fiction · Fantasy

To Whom This May Come 1898 was first published in the February issue of Harper's New Monthly Magazine.

The Angel of the Revolution: A Tale of the Coming Terror

The Angel of the Revolution: A Tale of the Coming Terror

by George Chetwynd Griffith

Science fiction · Fantasy · Novel

The Angel of the Revolution: A Tale of the Coming Terror was his first published novel and remains his most famous work. The Angel of the Revolution: A Tale of the Coming Terror was first published in Pearson's Weekly and was prompted by the success of "The Great War of " in Black and White magazine, which was itself inspired by The Battle of Dorking.

Khaled, A Tale of Arabia

Khaled, A Tale of Arabia

by F. Marion (Francis Marion) Crawford

1891 · Science fiction · Fantasy · Adventure

Khaled, A Tale of Arabia was first published in hardcover by Macmillan and Co. The Ballantine edition includes an introduction by Lin Carter.

The Invasion

The Invasion

by William Le Queux

Science fiction · Fantasy · Novel

The Invasion is one of the most famous examples of invasion literature. The Invasion is viewed by some as an example of pre-World War I Germanophobia.

In a Glass Darkly, v. 2/3

In a Glass Darkly, v. 2/3

by Joseph Sheridan Le Fanu

Science fiction · Mystery · Fantasy

In a Glass Darkly, v. 2/3: the second and third stories are revised versions of previously published stories. The first three stories are short stories, and the fourth and fifth are long enough to be called novellas (the fourth is over 44,500 words long, and the fifth is over 27,500 words long).

The World Masters

The World Masters

by George Chetwynd Griffith

Science fiction · Fantasy · Novel

The World Masters is one of the longest-running and most prestigious of the BDO/WDF tournaments. The World Masters was originally contested as the best of 5 legs (first to 3) before later transitioning to the set format.

Frankenstein; Or, The Modern Prometheus

Frankenstein; Or, The Modern Prometheus

by Mary Wollstonecraft Shelley

Science fiction · Gothic · Fantasy

Frankenstein; Or, The Modern Prometheus recounts Victor Frankenstein, a young scientist who creates a sapient creature from different body parts in an unorthodox scientific experiment. Shelley started writing the story when she was 18 and staying in Bath, and the first edition was published anonymously in London on 1 January, when she was 20.

Anno Domini 2000; or, Woman's Destiny

Anno Domini 2000; or, Woman's Destiny

by Julius, Sir Vogel

Science fiction · Fantasy · Novel

Anno Domini 2000; or, Woman's Destiny was written by former Prime Minister of New Zealand Sir Julius Vogel while he was in England.

The Mummy! A Tale of the Twenty-Second Century

The Mummy! A Tale of the Twenty-Second Century

by Mrs. (Jane) Loudon

Science fiction · Fantasy · Novel

The Mummy! A Tale of the Twenty-Second Century concerns the Egyptian mummy of Cheops, who is brought back to life in the year 2126. The Mummy! A Tale of the Twenty-Second Century describes a future filled with advanced technology, and was the first English-language story to feature a reanimated mummy.

The Battle of Dorking

The Battle of Dorking

by George Tomkyns Chesney

Science fiction · Fantasy · Novel

The Battle of Dorking: Reminiscences of a Volunteer is a novella by George Chesney, starting the genre of invasion literature and an important precursor of science fiction. Written just after the German victory in the Franco-Prussian War, it describes an invasion of Britain by a German-speaking country referred to in oblique terms as The Other Power or The Enemy.

A message from Mars

A message from Mars

by Richard Ganthony

Plays · Science fiction · Fantasy

A Message from Mars is a play by Richard Ganthony, first performed at London's Avenue Theatre in November.
