Short stories

Tales and collections you can finish in a sitting. Each entry links onward; this site does not keep the text.

The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

by Arthur Conan Doyle

1892 · Short stories · Mystery

Twelve early cases, from a speckled band to a scandal in Bohemia, narrated by Dr. Watson. This is the collection that fixed Holmes in the popular imagination.

A Christmas Carol

A Christmas Carol

by Charles Dickens

1843 · Short stories

Ebenezer Scrooge is walked through his past, the present he refuses to see, and a future he can still change. Dickens wrote it quickly, and it became the Christmas story people mean.

The Yellow Wallpaper

The Yellow Wallpaper

by Charlotte Perkins Gilman

1892 · Short stories · Gothic

A woman confined to a room for her health begins to watch the pattern in the wallpaper. Gilman’s story is brief and does not offer the cure the physician promised.

Heart of Darkness

Heart of Darkness

by Joseph Conrad

1899 · Short stories · Adventure

Marlow tells a deck audience about his trip upriver to find Kurtz, an ivory agent who has become a rumor. Conrad’s tale is about empire and about storytelling.

The Metamorphosis

The Metamorphosis

by Franz Kafka

1915 · Short stories

Gregor Samsa wakes as an insect and worries first about missing the train. The household’s patience, and then its accounting, are the rest of the story. The link is to a public-domain English translation.

Dubliners

Dubliners

by James Joyce

1914 · Short stories

Fifteen stories of Dublin life, ending with The Dead. Joyce called the method a kind of clarity; the characters often stop just short of changing.

Island Nights' Entertainments

Island Nights' Entertainments

by Robert Louis Stevenson

Short stories

Island Nights' Entertainments would prove to contain some of his final completed work before his death in 1894.

An Occurrence at Owl Creek Bridge

An Occurrence at Owl Creek Bridge

by Ambrose Bierce

Short stories · Gothic · History

An Occurrence at Owl Creek Bridge was originally published by the San Francisco Examiner and was first collected in Bierce's book Tales of Soldiers and Civilians. Bierce's abandonment of strict linear narration in favor of the internal mind of the protagonist is an early example of the stream-of-consciousness narrative mode.

Mosses from an old manse

Mosses from an old manse

by Nathaniel Hawthorne

Short stories · Gothic · Letters

Mosses from an Old Manse is a short story collection by Nathaniel Hawthorne, first published.

The Dynamiter

The Dynamiter

by Robert Louis Stevenson

Short stories · Mystery · Adventure

More New Arabian Nights: The Dynamiter is a gathering of linked short stories by Robert Louis Stevenson and Fanny van de Grift Stevenson.

The Amateur Cracksman

The Amateur Cracksman

by E. W. (Ernest William) Hornung

Short stories · Mystery

The Amateur Cracksman was published in the UK by Methuen & Co. London, and in the US by Scribner's, New York.

The Red One

The Red One

by Jack London

Short stories · Science fiction · Fantasy

The Red One was first published in the October issue of The Cosmopolitan, two years after London's death. The Red One was reprinted in the same year by MacMillan, in a gathering of London's stories of the same name.

A House of Pomegranates

A House of Pomegranates

by Oscar Wilde

Short stories · Fantasy · Children's books

A House of Pomegranates is Wilde's second fairy tale collection, following The Happy Prince and Other Tales. He said of the book that it was "intended neither for the British child nor the British public".

The Happy Prince, and Other Tales

The Happy Prince, and Other Tales

by Oscar Wilde

Short stories · Fantasy · Children's books

The Happy Prince, and Other Tales contains five stories that are highly popular among children and frequently read in schools: "The Happy Prince," "The Nightingale and the Rose," "The Selfish Giant," "The Devoted Friend," and "The Remarkable Rocket. " The short stories are valued for their morals, and have been made into animated films.

To Be Read at Dusk

To Be Read at Dusk

by Charles Dickens

Short stories · Mystery · Gothic

"To Be Read at Dusk" is a short story written by Charles Dickens, first published in The Keepsake.

Joe Wilson and His Mates

Joe Wilson and His Mates

by Henry Lawson

1902 · Short stories · History

Joe Wilson and His Mates was released in hardback by William Blackwood in when Lawson was living in England, and features one of the author's better known stories in "The Loaded Dog". The collection contains twenty stories which are mostly reprinted from a variety of newspaper and magazine sources, with several published here for the first time.

The Cask of Amontillado

The Cask of Amontillado

by Edgar Allan Poe

Short stories · Mystery · Gothic

The Cask of Amontillado, set in an unnamed Italian city at Carnival time, concerns a man taking fatal revenge on a friend who, he believes, has insulted him. Like several of Poe's stories, and in keeping with the 19th-century fascination with the subject, the narrative follows a person being buried alive – in this case, by immurement.

The Masque of the Red Death

The Masque of the Red Death

by Edgar Allan Poe

Short stories · Gothic

The Masque of the Red Death stays with Prince Prospero's attempts to avoid a dangerous plague, known as the Red Death, by hiding in his abbey. He, along with many other wealthy nobles, hosts a masquerade ball in seven rooms of the abbey, each decorated with a different color.

Kwaidan: Stories and Studies of Strange Things

Kwaidan: Stories and Studies of Strange Things

by Lafcadio Hearn

Short stories · Gothic · Fantasy

Kwaidan: Stories and Studies of Strange Things (怪談, Kaidan; also archaic romanized Kwaidan), often shortened to Kwaidan ("ghost story"), is a 1904 book by Koizumi Yakumo (Lafcadio Hearn) that features several Japanese ghost stories and a brief non-fiction study on insects.

A Message from the Sea

A Message from the Sea

by Charles Dickens

1860 · Short stories · Adventure

"A Message from the Sea" is a set of short stories by Charles Dickens, Wilkie Collins, Robert Buchanan, Charles Allston Collins, Amelia Edwards and Harriet Parr, written in 1860 for the Christmas issue of All the Year Round.

Tom Tiddler's Ground

Tom Tiddler's Ground

by Charles Dickens

Short stories

Tom Tiddler's ground, also known as Tom Tidler's ground or Tommy Tiddler's ground, is a longstanding children's game. One player, "Tom Tiddler", stands on a heap of stones, gravel, etc.

Doctor Marigold

Doctor Marigold

by Charles Dickens

Short stories · Children's books

Originally published, Doctor Marigold was extremely successful, as were Dickens's public performances of a play based on the story--fascinating and easy to read. Doctor (it is his given name) Marigold is a "Cheap Jack" or what we would call a street peddler.

My Buried Treasure

My Buried Treasure

by Richard Harding Davis

Short stories · Adventure · Humor

My Buried Treasure was published as a book, but is set in the 18th century, and tells a story of "buccaneers and buried gold". My Buried Treasure is considered a coming-of-age story, and is noted for its atmosphere, characters, and action.

The Messengers

The Messengers

by Richard Harding Davis

Short stories · Romance

The Messengers stars Kristen Stewart, John Corbett, William B. Davis, Dylan McDermott, Carter Kolbeck and Penelope Ann Miller.

A Set of Six

A Set of Six

by Joseph Conrad

Short stories · Adventure

The works were collected in A Set of Six, published by Methuen and Company.

A House to Let

A House to Let

by Charles Dickens

Short stories · Mystery

A House to Let was originally published the Christmas edition of Dickens's Household Words magazine. Collins wrote the introduction and collaborated with Dickens on the second story and ending, while Gaskell and Proctor wrote the third and fifth chapters respectively.

The Son of the Wolf

The Son of the Wolf

by Jack London

Short stories · Adventure

The Son of the Wolf is a northern set in Canada's Yukon and is based on a short story of the same name by Jack London.

Lost Face

Lost Face

by Jack London

Short stories · Adventure

Lost Face takes its name from the first short story in the book, about an European adventurer in the Yukon who outwits his (American) Indian captors' plans to torture him. Lost Face includes London's best-known short story, "To Build a Fire".

When God Laughs, and Other Stories

When God Laughs, and Other Stories

by Jack London

Short stories

Released, When God Laughs, and Other Stories is the eleventh gathering of short stories by Jack London. In contrast with most of his other work that had been released at the time, When God Laughs is set in Polynesia.

A Dog's Tale

A Dog's Tale

by Mark Twain

Short stories

A Dog's Tale first appeared in the December issue of Harper's Magazine. In January of the following year it was extracted into a stand-alone pamphlet published for the National Anti-Vivisection Society.

The Decameron, Volume I

The Decameron, Volume I

by Giovanni Boccaccio

Short stories · History

The Decameron ( dih-KAM-ər-ən; Italian: Decameron [deˈkaːmeron, dekameˈrɔn, -ˈron] or Decamerone [dekameˈroːne]), subtitled Prince Galehaut (Old Italian: Prencipe Galeotto [ˈprentʃipe ɡaleˈɔtto, ˈprɛn-]), is a gathering of short stories by the 14th-century Italian author Giovanni Boccaccio (1313–1375).

The Haunted Mind (From "Twice Told Tales")

The Haunted Mind (From "Twice Told Tales") was first published in The Token and Atlantic Souvenir,. The Haunted Mind (From "Twice Told Tales") was later included in Volume Two of Twice-Told Tales, a gathering of short stories by Hawthorne published.

A Virtuoso's Collection (From "Mosses from an Old Manse")

The Man of Adamant (From: "The Snow Image and Other Twice-Told Tales")

The Man of Adamant (From: "The Snow Image and Other Twice-Told Tales")

by Nathaniel Hawthorne

Short stories · History

The Man of Adamant (From: "The Snow Image and Other Twice-Told Tales") was first published in the edition of The Token and Atlantic Souvenir, edited by Samuel Griswold Goodrich. The Man of Adamant (From: "The Snow Image and Other Twice-Told Tales") later appeared in Hawthorne's final gathering of short stories The Snow-Image, and Other Twice-Told Tales, published by Ticknor, Reed & Fields.

Struwwelpeter: Merry Stories and Funny Pictures

Struwwelpeter: Merry Stories and Funny Pictures

by Heinrich Hoffmann

Poetry · Short stories · Children's books

Struwwelpeter: Merry Stories and Funny Pictures comprises ten illustrated and rhymed stories, mostly about children. Each cautionary tale has a clear moral lesson that demonstrates the disastrous consequences of misbehavior in an exaggerated way.

A Country Doctor and Selected Stories and Sketches

A Country Doctor and Selected Stories and Sketches

by Sarah Orne Jewett

Short stories

A Country Doctor and Selected Stories and Sketches, which was first published, was based on the relationship between Jewett and her physician father.

The Man Without a Country, and Other Tales

The Man Without a Country, and Other Tales

by Edward Everett Hale

Short stories · Adventure · History

The Man Without a Country, and Other Tales recounts a young American officer who declares himself disgusted with his country during a trial for treason, and wishes he never hears about her ever again. He is sentenced to spend the rest of his days at sea without so much as a word of news about the United States.

The Mummy's Foot

The Mummy's Foot

by Théophile Gautier

Short stories · Science fiction · Gothic

The Mummy's Foot relates the fantastical tale of a contemporary man and the adventures which befall him when he ventures into a Parisian curiosity shop and buys the four-thousand-year-old foot of Princess Hermonthis.

To Whom This May Come 1898

To Whom This May Come 1898

Short stories · Science fiction · Fantasy

To Whom This May Come 1898 was first published in the February issue of Harper's New Monthly Magazine.

"The Snow-Image: A Childish Miracle" is a short story by the American writer Nathaniel Hawthorne, which gave its title to his collection The Snow-Image, and Other Twice-Told Tales, published.
