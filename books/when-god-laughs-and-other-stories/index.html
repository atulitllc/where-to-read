Jack London (1876–1916) is the writer named on When God Laughs, and Other Stories. The book comes from that life, which is why this catalog treats the text as public domain in the United States. About the author

London’s Northland books put animals and people under the same pressure: cold, hunger, and the stories civilization tells about both. The Call of the Wild follows a dog out of California and into that pressure. More of this shelf for Jack London.
