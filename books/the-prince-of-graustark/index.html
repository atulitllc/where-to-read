by George Barr McCutcheon

1914 · Romance · Humor · Novel

In The Prince of Graustark, Graustark is a fictional country in Eastern Europe used as a setting for several novels by George Barr McCutcheon. Graustark's neighbors, which also figure in the stories, are Axphain to the north and Dawsbergen to the south.
