The Raven

by Edgar Allan Poe

Poetry · Fantasy

Open Library OL41081W

In The Raven, First published in January, the poem is often noted for its musicality, stylized language and supernatural atmosphere. The Raven tells of a distraught lover who is paid a visit by a mysterious raven that repeatedly speaks a single word: "nevermore".
