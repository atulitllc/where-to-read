Devil worship

by Isya, -1923 Joseph

1919 · History · Religion

Open Library OL249923W

The Devil Worshippers (German: Die Teufelsanbeter) is a six-chapter silent German film written by Marie Luise Droop, directed by Muhsin Ertuğrul and featuring Carl de Vogt in the title-role of Kara Ben Nemsi. De Vogt's career as an actor stretched into the 1960s, where he appeared in a number of the then-popular German crimi films.
