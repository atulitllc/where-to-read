Measure for Measure

by William Shakespeare

Plays

Open Library OL259017W

In Measure for Measure, the play turns on the despotic and puritan Angelo, a deputy entrusted to rule the city of Vienna in the absence of Duke Vincentio, who instead disguises himself as a humble friar to observe Angelo's regency and his citizens' lives.
