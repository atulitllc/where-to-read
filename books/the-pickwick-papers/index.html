Charles Dickens (1812–1870) is the writer named on The Pickwick Papers. The book comes from that life, which is why this catalog treats the text as public domain in the United States. It is filed here as humor and novel. Those words are a guide to the shelf, not a summary standing in for the book, and not a claim that one label exhausts it. A finding aid can give you the name, the date, and a door. The reading itself stays on Open Library.

About the author

Dickens filled novels with institutions, comic speech, and people the city would rather not count. The books here run from a short Christmas tale to the long arc of Great Expectations. More of this shelf for Charles Dickens.
