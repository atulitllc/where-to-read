The Yellow Wallpaper

by Charlotte Perkins Gilman

1892 · Short stories · Gothic

Open Library OL2772007W

A woman confined to a room for her health begins to watch the pattern in the wallpaper. Gilman’s story is brief and does not offer the cure the physician promised.

The date on The Yellow Wallpaper is 1892, and the author credit is Charlotte Perkins Gilman (1860–1935). Subject shelves for the page are Short stories and Gothic. Identifiers that keep this page distinct are Open Library OL2772007W and Gutenberg 1952.

About the author

Gilman wrote The Yellow Wallpaper after a physician prescribed her a rest that forbade work. The story is short, domestic, and relentless about what that kind of care does to a mind. More of this shelf for Charlotte Perkins Gilman.
