The Warfare of Science

by Andrew Dickson White

1876 · History · Religion · Science

Open Library OL1461761W

A History of the Warfare of Science with Theology in Christendom was published within the United States in two volumes by Andrew Dickson White, a founder of Cornell University,. White chronicles the gradual emancipation of science from theology in various fields.
