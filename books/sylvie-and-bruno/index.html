Lewis Carroll’s Sylvie and Bruno sits on this shelf as a public-domain science fiction. Treat the note as a finding aid, not as a jacket summary and not as a substitute for the book. Follow the Open Library work rather than expecting a copy stored on this site.

Lewis Carroll (1832–1898) is the writer named on Sylvie and Bruno. The book comes from that life, which is why this catalog treats the text as public domain in the United States. It is filed here as science fiction, fantasy and children's books. Those words are a guide to the shelf, not a summary standing in for the book, and not a claim that one label exhausts it. A finding aid can give you the name, the date, and a door. The reading itself stays on Open Library.

About the author

Carroll, the pen name of Charles Dodgson, sent Alice through a world that keeps changing the rules. The jokes are logical as often as they are whimsical. More of this shelf for Lewis Carroll.
