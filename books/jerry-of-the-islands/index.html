Jack London (1876–1916) is the writer named on Jerry of the Islands. The book comes from that life, which is why this catalog treats the text as public domain in the United States. It is filed here as adventure and novel. Those words are a guide to the shelf, not a summary standing in for the book, and not a claim that one label exhausts it. About the author

London’s Northland books put animals and people under the same pressure: cold, hunger, and the stories civilization tells about both. The Call of the Wild follows a dog out of California and into that pressure. More of this shelf for Jack London.
