In Maria; Or, The Wrongs of Woman, Maria: or, The Wrongs of Woman is Mary Wollstonecraft's unfinished novelistic sequel to her revolutionary political treatise A Vindication of the Rights of Woman. The Wrongs of Woman was published posthumously in by her husband, William Godwin, and is often considered her most radical feminist work.
