Alice's Adventures in Wonderland

by Lewis Carroll

1865 · Children's books

Open Library OL138052W

Alice follows a rabbit and finds a world that argues with her about size, manners, and the meaning of words. Carroll wrote it for a real child and then built a classic out of the joke.

Alice's Adventures in Wonderland is listed with the date 1865. Lewis Carroll (1832–1898) is the writer named on it, and the text is in the public domain in the United States. About the author

Carroll, the pen name of Charles Dodgson, sent Alice through a world that keeps changing the rules. The jokes are logical as often as they are whimsical. More of this shelf for Lewis Carroll.
