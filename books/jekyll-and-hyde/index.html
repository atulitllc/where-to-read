The Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde

by Robert Louis Stevenson

1886 · Gothic · Novel

Open Library OL24156W

A London lawyer tries to understand the tie between a respectable doctor and a violent man who shares his house. Stevenson’s short novel is a study of a self split on purpose.

The Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde is listed with the date 1886. Robert Louis Stevenson (1850–1894) is the writer named on it, and the text is in the public domain in the United States. About the author

Stevenson could write a straight adventure and a nightmare about a divided self in the same decade. Treasure Island and Jekyll and Hyde are both exact about consequence. More of this shelf for Robert Louis Stevenson.
