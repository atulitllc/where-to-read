Mysteries of Paris — Volume 02

by Eugène Sue

Novel

Open Library OL41283254W

Mysteries of Paris was published serially in 90 parts in Journal des débats from 19 June until 15 October, making it one of the first serial novels (feuilleton) published in France. Mysteries of Paris recounts a mysterious man named Rodolphe, who seeks to restore social justice in 19th-century Paris and comes to the aid of various characters, including a prostitute and a criminal.
